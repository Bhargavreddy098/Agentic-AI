# Local-first agent workspace (working description)

- What it is: A premium local-first application to configure, run, observe, and control autonomous AI agents across model providers.
- Who it is for: Initially AI power users, developers, indie hackers, technical founders, and researchers using coding agents, general agents, and local LLM tools.
- What it sells, at what price: Agent execution and management software. Monetization and willingness to pay are explicitly open questions; options include an open-source local core with paid Pro, paid desktop software, subscriptions, BYOK, and optional future cloud services. No price selected.
- Where and how: Delivered primarily on the customer's computer, with local data where practical and local/cloud models. Proposed discovery through GitHub, developer communities, X, Reddit, AI communities, YouTube, Product Hunt, and task demonstrations.
- Budget and constraints: Small team; budget and runway unspecified. Local-first and provider independence matter. Avoid unnecessary enterprise, team, RBAC, or multitenant scope. Premium UX and genuine autonomous execution required.
